## Sponsor Us

GAPC is organised entirely by student volunteers, and sponsors are what make it possible. With your support we can keep the contest free to enter, feed a room full of hungry programmers, and hand out prizes worth competing for.

In return, your company gets in front of some of the most talented algorithmic thinkers in the region: students who spend their free time solving hard problems under pressure. We're happy to work out a partnership that fits what your company is looking for.

Interested? Send us an email at [programming_committee@svcover.nl](mailto:programming_committee@svcover.nl) and we'll get back to you.
