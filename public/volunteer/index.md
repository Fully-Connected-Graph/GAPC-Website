## Help Us Run GAPC 2027

GAPC runs on people. Behind every smooth contest day there's a crew making sure teams find their seats, laptops work, food shows up on time and nobody gets lost on the way to the Study Landscape. We'd love for you to be part of that crew.

No competitive programming experience needed. If you're friendly and happy to lend a hand for (part of) the day, you're exactly who we're looking for.

<div class="vol-grid">
  <div class="vol-card">
    <div class="vol-title">Set up</div>
    <p>Get the room ready in the morning: tables, laptops, signs and seat numbers.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="vol-card">
    <div class="vol-title">Welcome</div>
    <p>Greet teams as they arrive and help them find their way.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="vol-card">
    <div class="vol-title">Support</div>
    <p>Be around during the contest to help contestants with practical questions.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="vol-card">
    <div class="vol-title">Food &amp; wrap-up</div>
    <p>Help out with lunch, snacks and pizza, and tidy up at the end of the day.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Why Volunteer?

- Get a nice GAPC 2027 t-shirt to wear on the day (and keep afterwards).
- See a programming contest from behind the scenes.
- Meet the contestants, the organisers and our partners.
- Gain hands-on experience organising a real event.
- Help make GAPC a great day for everyone taking part.
